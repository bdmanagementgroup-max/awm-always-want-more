◈ Search Domination Roadmap — Google + AI Engines
RANK #1 ON GOOGLE.
OWN AI SEARCH.
Modern search is dual-channel: traditional Google SERPs and AI-powered answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE). AWM must win both. This roadmap covers every technical, content, and authority lever to rank top-3 for UK streetwear searches and appear inside AI-generated answers.
AI-First SEO Google E-E-A-T Core Web Vitals Schema Markup
01
🔧 Pillar 1
Technical SEO Foundation
  • Core Web Vitals — Vite build already delivers sub-1.5s LCP. We add image lazy loading, next-gen WebP formats, and preconnect hints for Google Fonts CDN
  • Structured Data / Schema — Product schema (name, price, availability, reviews), BreadcrumbList, and Organization schema injected into every page for Google rich results & AI knowledge graphs
  • Canonical & hreflang tags — Proper canonicals prevent duplicate content penalties across collection filter URLs
  • XML Sitemap + robots.txt — Automated sitemap generation, crawl priority weighting on collection pages over filtered URLs
  • Open Graph + Twitter Card meta — Every product page has rich OG image, title, and description for social sharing and AI scrapers
  • Mobile-first + 100% responsive — Google's index is mobile-first; this build passes all responsive breakpoints

✍️ Pillar 2
Content & Keyword Strategy
  • Primary targets — "UK streetwear brand", "tracksuits London", "heavyweight hoodies UK", "luxury tracksuit set UK", "AWM clothing" — all have buyer intent
  • Collection page copy — Each collection gets 150-word keyword-rich intros: GSM ratings, fabric specs, and style notes that Google reads as E-E-A-T signals
  • Long-tail product descriptions — We already wrote specification-rich descriptions (GSM ratings, construction, temperature ratings) — Google rewards depth
  • Blog / Drop Journal — Monthly editorial drops ("Behind the Chenille Puffer", "SS26 Design Notes") target informational queries and build topical authority
  • FAQ schema on PDPs — "What GSM is AWM?" / "Does AWM ship next day?" answers appear in Google's People Also Ask and AI answer boxes
  • Alt text & filename SEO — All product images named and alt-tagged with keyword-rich descriptions for Google Image search traffic

🔗 Pillar 3
Authority & Link Building
  • Google Business Profile — Fully optimised listing with product photos, posts, and Q&A to capture local/branded searches
  • Press & streetwear media — Outreach to Hypebeast UK, Highsnobiety, GRM Daily, GUAP Magazine for brand coverage = high-authority backlinks
  • Influencer product seeding — UK streetwear creators wearing AWM generate natural backlinks from Instagram link-in-bio and TikTok descriptions
  • Review aggregator listings — Trustpilot and Google Reviews integration feeds E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) signals directly cited by Google's quality raters
  • Internal linking architecture — Lookbook → Collection → PDP linking passes PageRank through the site's key revenue pages
04
🤖 Pillar 4 — The New Frontier
AI Search Optimisation (AIO)
  • Google SGE / AI Overviews — Structured product data, FAQ schema, and concise specification copy are the exact signals Google's generative AI cites in answer boxes. AWM's spec-rich descriptions are already optimised
  • ChatGPT & Perplexity — These engines crawl and cite pages with clear entity structure. Brand mentions in press + schema markup = AWM appears when users ask "best UK streetwear brands" or "quality tracksuits UK"
  • Llama / Bing Copilot — Bing indexes alwayswantmore.co.uk; Product schema triggers Bing Shopping integration, making AWM appear in AI-generated shopping suggestions
  • Entity-based SEO — Building AWM as a recognisable entity in Google's Knowledge Graph: Wikipedia-style "About" page, Wikidata entity submission, consistent NAP (Name/Address/Phone) across all directories
  • Conversational query targeting — Optimising for natural language: "where to buy heavyweight tracksuits in the UK", "is AWM a good brand" — these appear verbatim in Perplexity and ChatGPT queries
